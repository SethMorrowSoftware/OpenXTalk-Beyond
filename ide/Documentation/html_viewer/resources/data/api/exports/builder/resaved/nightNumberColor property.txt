nightNumberColor
(property)
Determines the color of the numbers on the clock during the night

Syntax:
get the nightNumberColor of {widget}
----
set the nightNumberColor of {widget} to {color}
----

Synonyms:

Params:

Examples:
Description:
Use the nightNumberColor property to get or set the text color of the numbers on the face of the clock during the night. To determine if the clock is displaying its daytime or night-time colors use the read-only property isDay.
