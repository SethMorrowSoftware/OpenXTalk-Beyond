textAlign
(property)
Specifies how text is aligned or justified in a field or button.

Syntax:
set the textAlign [of {line}] of {{button} | {field}} to {left | center | right}
----

Synonyms:

Params:

Examples:
set the textAlign of field "myField" to center
----
set the textAlign of line 1 of field "myField" to right
----

Description:
Use the textAlign property to change the alignment of individual lines of text in a field, the whole field or button|buttons.

By default, the textAlign of newly created fields and buttons is "left". 

Values:

OS:
mac,windows,linux,ios,android
