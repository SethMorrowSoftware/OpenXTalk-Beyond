object hierarchy
(glossary)

Syntax:
Synonyms:
object hierarchy, object hierarchies

Params:

Examples:
Description:
The chain of ownership in which one object(glossary) is owner|owned by another. A card control is owner|owned by the card it's on; a grouped control are owner|owned by its group; a card is owned by the stack it's in; and substack|substacks are owner|owned by their main stack.

Also, the chain of all the object|objects above a specific object(glossary) in the hierarchy.
