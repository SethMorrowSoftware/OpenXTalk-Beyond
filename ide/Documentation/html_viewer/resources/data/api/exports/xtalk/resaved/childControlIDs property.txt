childControlIDs
(property)
Reports the IDs of all the children of a group.

Syntax:
get the childControlIDs of {group}
----

Synonyms:

Params:
group ( ): A reference to a group

Examples:
put the childControlIDs of group "list"
----

Description:
Use the childControlIDs property to get a list of the short ids of all the direct children of a group  Each line of the childControlIDs is the short id of a control which is a direct child of the group, the property does not recurse into subgroups. 

Values:
The childControlIDs of a group reports a list of the short id properties of the direct children of a group. This property is read-only and cannot be set. 

OS:
mac,windows,linux,ios,android
