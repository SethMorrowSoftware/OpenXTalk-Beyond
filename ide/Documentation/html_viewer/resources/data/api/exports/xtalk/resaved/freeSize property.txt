freeSize
(property)
Always reports zero and is included for compatibility with imported HyperCard stacks.

Syntax:
get the freeSize of {stack}
----

Synonyms:

Params:

Examples:
Description:
In HyperCard, the freeSize property reports the amount of wasted space in a stack. In OpenXTalk, this property always reports zero.
