- Android: on Android 15 and newer, a standalone no longer has the bottom of its stack under the navigation bar. The engine took the navigation bar, which Android 15 draws the app under, for the keyboard, so it told the stack the keyboard showed at launch and sized the stack to the whole screen below the status bar; where the stack is laid out by the window's insets it now asks them whether the keyboard shows, and older Android and other windows are measured as before (found with the Android stack player on an Android 15 emulator; affects 0.2.7 to 0.2.8-dp.2) (#584)
- Tests: tools/ci/tests/test_android_java.py holds the keyboard check to the insets where the stack is laid out by them and to LiveCode's sum everywhere else, and android_java.py's compare names the change against every released Android Java layer (#584)
