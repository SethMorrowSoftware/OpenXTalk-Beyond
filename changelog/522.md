- HyperCard import: the HyperCard Library does what the sound externals did, where their stand-ins did nothing: FPlay plays the stack's sound of that name (a number names "snd <id>"; "stop" stops it), FSound() gives "done" when no sound plays, so a stack's "wait until fsound() is "done"" no longer waits for ever, and SoundCapToRes makes a SoundCap or SoundWave file a sound of the stack it names; of the 138 stacks of the corpus that carry or call one of these or the PICT writers, 2 fewer stop on an error, 2 fewer time out (The Space Stack's two versions) and none newly does; stacks imported before keep the library they were given
- HyperCard import: the HyperCard Library does what the PICT writers did, for pictures in a format this engine reads (PNG, GIF, JPEG, BMP): ClipToPICT() and PICTFileToRes() make the clipboard's picture or a file's a picture of the stack, which DispPICT and picture show, and give its id; the command ClipToPICT writes the clipboard's picture to a file, and PICTToClip puts a file's picture on the clipboard. A Mac PICT file is not read, and the result says so
