- Home: a long frame cannot turn the Move page's sparks round. Their drag is "speed * (1 - sDt * 1.2)", and since frames are paced on a slow computer a frame moves the animations on by up to 0.75 of a second, which leaves a tenth of the speed; past 0.83 of a second the factor would be less than nothing and a spark would fly back the way it came, so the drag now holds at nothing. With the cap as it is no frame draws differently; test_home_speed holds every such factor in Home, the Learn kit and Learn to "max(0, ...)" and checks that the longest frame the cap allows still leaves some of the speed (#454)
