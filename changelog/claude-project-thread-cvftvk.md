- Home, the Academy and Learn: on a computer that takes longer than 16 milliseconds to draw one frame of their animations, they wait as long after each frame as it took, so they keep about half of the time for clicks, keys and the user's own stacks, not four fifths or more; the animations move as far in fewer frames. A computer that draws a frame in 16 milliseconds or less draws as many frames as before (seth) (#436)
