- Beyond Beats, a pattern studio in the Stack Player grown from Beat Box (which stays as it was): up to twelve channels of a sixteen-sound kit that the browser makes, sixteen patterns of 16 or 32 steps with a velocity on every step, volume, pan, mute and solo on every channel, a tempo and a swing, kept in time by Web Audio and saved in the browser; its first phase, with song mode, a piano roll, effects and export to come (#582)
- Tests: tests/lcs/webplayer/beyond-beats.livecodescript (18 tests of the song, the screen and the file), tools/ci/tests/test_beyond_beats.py, and tools/ci/beyond_beats_check.py, run by the Stack Player's check, which renders the audio core's songs offline and measures them sample by sample and plays the stack in the bare player (#582)
