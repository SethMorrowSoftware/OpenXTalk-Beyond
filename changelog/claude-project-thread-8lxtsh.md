- Beyond Beats, a beat studio in the Stack Player grown from Beat Box (which stays as it was): a channel rack of up to twelve channels and sixteen patterns of 16 or 32 steps with a velocity on every step; a playlist of eight tracks and 128 bars with Pattern and Song modes; a piano roll for every channel; a mixer with a filter, reverb and delay sends, pan, a fader and a meter on every channel; a kit of sixteen sounds and five synths that the browser makes, and up to eight samples of the user's own; undo and redo; songs kept in the browser, saved to and opened from a file, and exported as WAV, all kept in time by Web Audio (#582)
- Tests: tests/lcs/webplayer/beyond-beats.livecodescript (43 tests of the song, the views, the screen and the files), tools/ci/tests/test_beyond_beats.py, and tools/ci/beyond_beats_check.py, run by the Stack Player's check, which renders the audio core's songs offline and measures them sample by sample and plays the stack in the bare player (#582)
