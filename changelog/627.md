- Files: `move file <path> to trash` (and `move folder`) puts a file or folder in the user's trash, and `show <path> in finder` (or `in explorer`) shows it in the desktop's file manager, from HyperXTalk (Emily-Elizabeth Howard); "trash", "finder" and "explorer" are no words of the language, and `show <path> in finder`, which compiled before as `show <path>` and stopped when it ran, now shows the file; `show tVar in finder` with tVar holding an object's name, which showed the object and then stopped, now gives "file not found" (#627)
- Fields: the markdownText of a field, or of a chunk of one, gives and sets its text as Markdown, from HyperXTalk (Emily-Elizabeth Howard), with its reader's hang on a lone backslash and its lost characters fixed, and lines of code written as one fenced block and a list's items line by line, so they come back as they were; a field that holds a custom property "markdownText", and every object that is not a field, keeps it as before, while a field that holds none gives its Markdown where it gave empty, and setting it fills the field where it made a custom property (#627)
- Arrays: a word of the language can be a key in brackets (`tArray[from]`, `tArray[to]`), from HyperXTalk (Emily-Elizabeth Howard); such keys failed to compile before, and every key that compiled gives what it gave (#627)
- Security: randomBytes no longer needs the security library, from HyperXTalk (Stam Kapetanakis); where it threw "can't load security library" it now gives the bytes (#627)
- Widgets: dropShadow, innerShadow, outerGlow, innerGlow and colorOverlay are drawn on widgets, from HyperXTalk (Stam Kapetanakis); a widget whose module declares one, or that holds a custom property of that name, keeps it as before, and on other widgets setting one no longer makes a custom property (#627)
- Script Editor: a list of completions beside the caret (the language's words and the message path's handlers, by their start or their humps), from HyperXTalk (Emily-Elizabeth Howard and Stam Kapetanakis), off until Edit > Options > Autocomplete is ticked (#627)
- Externals: an external in a folder of its own (`Externals/blur/blur.so`, or laid out as an extension's code) is loaded, from HyperXTalk (Stam Kapetanakis) (#627)
- Splash screen: it moves to the screen the IDE's menu bar was last on, from HyperXTalk (Emily-Elizabeth Howard) (#627)
- Property Inspector: a click into a custom property's value no longer selects the whole value, from HyperXTalk (BerndN) (#627)
