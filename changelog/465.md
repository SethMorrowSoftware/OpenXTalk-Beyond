- Windows: standard buttons, option menus and combo boxes follow a switch between the light and dark appearance that Windows did not announce to the engine; before, they kept the colours of the appearance the engine started in until Windows' notice came, which could leave a light button under the dark appearance's white words (a white button with white text was reported by richmond62). The engine now brings its colours, the title bars and every window up to date as soon as it reads the changed setting, and sends systemAppearanceChanged then too, so one change can send that message once more than before (#465)
- Windows check: a job with visual styles on Windows Server 2022 and 2025 switches Windows between light and dark, with and without its notice, and measures a button, an option menu and a combo box in the standalone engine; the same check can be run on a published release (#465)
