- Animated GIFs: a frame's transparent pixels leave what the frames before it drew, as browsers show it; they punched clear holes after every frame that was not "do not dispose" (no disposal given, which most GIF makers write, restore to background, restore to previous), so optimised GIFs flashed or showed holes (OpenXTalkPaul, p=26315) (#505)
- Animated GIFs: a "restore to previous" frame is restored on a canvas taller than wide (the check compared its height with a width, skipping the restore, and on a tall, narrow canvas could paste past the canvas's last row), and a frame that reaches past the canvas is drawn where it is on it instead of not at all; its rectangle is clipped to the canvas before restore to previous copies it, which read past the canvas; the canvas starts clear, where it started with whatever the allocator gave (#505)
- Images: the frameDurations (each frame's milliseconds, from the GIF's delays; set it to give frames your own) and the playRate (how fast an animated GIF plays: 2 twice as fast, 0.5 half as fast) of an image. Neither is saved with the stack, kept by a paste from the clipboard, or among its properties: without them a GIF plays at its own pace, as before (OpenXTalkPaul, p=26315) (#505)
- Tests: core/interface/animated-gif covers the three decoding fixes with GIFs of a few pixels (made by _animated-gif-fixtures.py) and the two properties (#505)
