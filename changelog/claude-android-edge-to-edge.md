- Android: a stack can be drawn edge to edge, as current Android apps are: tick the new **Edge to edge** box at the end of the Android card's Status Bar row (the "android,edge to edge" setting). On Android 11 and newer the app then fills the screen under the see-through status and navigation bars and into the display's cutout, the bars' icons follow the light or dark appearance, `the screenRect` is the whole of its window (the whole screen unless it shares it), and `the effective working screenRect` is the part the bars, the cutout and the keyboard leave uncovered. It needs runtimes whose Java layer can do it (made after 0.2.7-rc.6.19); with older ones the build stops and says so. A stack that does not tick it is built and laid out as before (#432)
- Android builder check: an edge probe (`ide --probe edge`) and `device --edge-to-edge` check on both emulators that a stack asking for it is drawn under the status bar and that its effective working screenRect leaves the bars out; the Java class check no longer reads the setting's meta-data name as a missing class; the IDE tour clicks the new box (#432)
