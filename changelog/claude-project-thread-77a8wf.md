- Dark appearance: the words of a field or a button with no text colour of its own are black on a light background colour that the stack gives it, its group or its card, as LiveCode Community drew them. They were the appearance's white, so a field with a white background showed white words on white (0.2.8-dp.1 on Windows 11, in the xTalk Suite's xlsxt and pdfxt demos and in any older stack that colours its fields). In the dark appearance such a control's effective foregroundColor is now the 0,0,0 its words are drawn in, where it was 255,255,255. Nothing changes in the light appearance, on a dark colour, for words with a colour of their own or one they inherit, for selected or disabled words, for menus, or in a stack that sets no colours; nothing is written to the stack (MCObject::getlegacywords) (#608)
- CI: the Windows dark switch check also draws an older stack's controls, none with a text colour: a field with a white background, a field in a light group, a light button, a checked check box with a white background and a field with a dark one, and checks their words and their effective foregroundColor in both appearances (tools/ci/dark_switch_check.py); tools/ci/tests/test_legacy_words.py holds the engine's rule to the way fields and buttons are filled (#608)
