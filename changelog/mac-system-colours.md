- macOS: a stack or card with no colours of its own follows the Mac's own colours, light or dark, and changes as soon as the Mac switches between them. In the dark appearance it is drawn in the Mac's window colour instead of a fixed 61,61,61, and the effective backgroundColor and foregroundColor of a stack or card (and of any control without colours of its own) read the colours of the appearance in use, where before they kept those of the appearance the IDE or standalone started in. The light appearance looks and reads as before; a stack or card printed in the dark appearance takes the light one's colours. The classic toolbar leaves its colour to the engine so that it matches the other palettes (bwmilby, openxtalk.org p=25665, p=25668; seth's choice 1A) (#PR)
