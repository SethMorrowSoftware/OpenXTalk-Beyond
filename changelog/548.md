- Ember Bricks' Online Duel opens on a lobby, so that two players no longer have to pass two codes by hand inside a minute: a player gives a name and goes online, sees who else is there and taps one to challenge them, takes a QUICK MATCH with the first other player who asks for one, or makes a room for a friend with PLAY A FRIEND and sends its link or its six letters; a match over, both are back in the lobby. It needs no server of the game's: the players meet on public Nostr relays (`wss://nos.lol`, `wss://relay.damus.io`, `wss://nostr.mom`, xTalk Chat's) through NostrXT, with a key made for the visit and messages of the ephemeral kinds, which relays pass on and are not meant to keep, and nothing is sent before the player chooses to go online, on a step that says what that shows. It plays in the Stack Player, the web IDE and OXT-Beyond on a desktop. The swap of two codes is still there, one tap further, as PLAY BY CODE, and is what the duel opens on wherever there is no NostrXT; a game by code and a game from the lobby make the same link (#548)
- Tests: sixteen more tests of the lobby in tests/lcs/webplayer/ember-bricks.livecodescript (the test is the other players), source tests of it in tools/ci/tests/test_ember_bricks.py, and tools/ci/ember_bricks_check.py now also plays the lobby in web.yml between three browser pages on a Nostr relay of its own on 127.0.0.1: a name, who is there, a challenge accepted and played over a real WebRTC link, a quick match, and a friend's room joined by its link. That relay (tools/ci/nostr_test_relay.py, `ephemeral`) keeps for five minutes what it passes on, as some public relays do, and the game takes only what is new. tools/ci/mobile-web-deploy.livecodescript takes script libraries to include (`OXT_MW_LIBRARIES`), and none unless asked (#548)
