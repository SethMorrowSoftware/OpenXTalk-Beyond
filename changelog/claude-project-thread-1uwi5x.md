- SQLite 3.54.1 (was 3.51.3) for the SQLite database driver and the web engine, with the fixes of the 3.53 and 3.54 patch releases. Values come back as before: the driver keeps 15 significant digits on every connection (SQLITE_DBCONFIG_FP_DIGITS), and a small change in sqlite3.c makes quote() and the JSON functions follow it. What remains different: quote() of a real that 15 digits cannot hold writes 18 digits in the exponent form instead of 19 (both read back to the same number), and the 15th digit of a real above about 1e29 or below about 1e-29 is now rounded correctly where 3.51 was sometimes one off (#PR)
