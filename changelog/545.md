- Debugger: after a breakpoint is dropped because its control was deleted (or because it was saved twice), the breakpoints after it in the stack keep their own on or off state and their own condition. Each took those of the breakpoint before it, so a breakpoint that was off could read as on in the Script Editor, and a conditional one could stop, or fail, on a condition written for another handler. The same for watches: the ones after a dropped watch keep their own state. What a script can see: nothing but the IDE's own record of breakpoints and watches in a stack that had one for a deleted control (#545)
- Tests: tests/lcs/debugger/stalebreakpoints gains three tests, of a dropped breakpoint, a breakpoint saved twice and a dropped watch (#545)
