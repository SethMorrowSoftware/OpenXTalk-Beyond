- Extensions: the SVG Drawing widget (org.openxtalk.widget.svgdrawing) shows a whole SVG, every shape in its own fill, stroke and gradient, scaled to the widget with its proportions kept or stretched (maintainAspectRatio). Set its svgText, which the drawing library compiles, or its drawing, compiled already; shapes painted with currentColor take its foregroundColor, and formattedWidth and formattedHeight give the SVG's own size. It saves the compiled drawing, so a saved stack, or a standalone, shows the picture without the drawing library. It is a new widget: no existing control or stack changes (asked for on the forum by OpenXTalkPaul) (#579)
- Tests: extensions/widgets/svgdrawing/tests/properties checks the widget's pixels for each colour of an SVG, the kept and stretched proportions, currentColor, a compile error that keeps the picture, values that are not drawings, and a saved stack drawn after the drawing library is gone (#579)
