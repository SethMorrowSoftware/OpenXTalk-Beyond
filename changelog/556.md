- Ember Bricks: while a level is played with a mouse, the mouse's pointer is hidden over the arena, on a desktop and on the web, so that it no longer sits on the paddle and the ball. It is back as soon as the level is not being played (on Pause, in the menus and the lobby, when a level is cleared or lost), over the Pause button and the score, and when the mouse leaves the arena or the window. The engine has one pointer for all its windows, so the game asks for it on every frame, and a hidden pointer comes back by itself 0.4 seconds after the last frame: a game that stops (a script error, the debugger) cannot leave the IDE without a pointer. A pointer another script has locked is left alone, a tool other than the browse tool keeps its own, and nothing changes for a finger (#556)
