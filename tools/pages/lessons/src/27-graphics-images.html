Shapes

A graphic is a shape the engine draws: it stays sharp at any size, and you can change it at any time by setting its properties. Its style is one of rectangle, roundRect, oval, line, polygon, curve and regular (a regular polygon).

A shape is filled with its backgroundColor when its filled is true, and drawn round with its foregroundColor (or borderColor) at its lineSize. Set the lineSize to 0 for a shape with no outline.

Gradients and see-through colours

A graphic can be filled with a gradient instead of one colour. Its fillGradient is an array: the type (linear, radial and others), the ramp of colours, each line a position from 0 to 1 followed by red, green and blue, and the points it runs from and to, with via giving its other direction.

The blendLevel of any control makes it see-through: 0 is solid, 100 invisible.

Images

An image holds pixels: a photo, an icon, a drawing. You usually bring one in with File > Import As Control > Image File, or set an image's filename to a file. A script can also paint one: its imageData is four bytes for every pixel, row by row, the last three of each being red, green and blue.

That is 128 times 128 pixels, at four bytes each. Each pixel's red grows from left to right and its green from top to bottom, so the corners show the four mixes.

export snapshot turns anything on a card into an image or a PNG file, which is how the pictures of the stage in these lessons are made.
