# Ember Bricks: the levels, in the order they are played.
#
# A level is a line "= Name" and then its rows, top row first, each of
# exactly 10 places and at most 12 of them; a blank line ends it. The
# builder (ember-bricks.livecodescript) puts this file in the stack as it
# is, and the game reads it as it opens; tools/ci/tests/test_ember_bricks.py
# holds every level to these rules.
#
#   .       nothing
#   1 to 7  a brick, one hit: red, orange, amber, green, cyan, blue, violet
#   T       a tough brick, two hits
#   U       an armoured brick, three hits
#   X       steel: no ball breaks it (a bomb does), and a level is clear
#           without it
#   B       a bomb: it takes the eight bricks around it with it, and their
#           bombs go off in turn
#   E       an ember: it always drops a spark worth catching
#
# The levels were drawn for this game. After the last one they come round
# again, faster, with some of their bricks made tough.
